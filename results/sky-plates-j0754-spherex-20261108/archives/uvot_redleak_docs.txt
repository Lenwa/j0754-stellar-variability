<h1>Red Leak</h1>
<em>Updated September 21, 2010</em>

<p>
     The transmission curves of the <i>Swift</i>/UVOT UVW1 and UVW2
filters have extended red tails.  This means that the flux measured
with these filters will be a combination of the ultraviolet flux from
the source and a contribution from the source's optical flux.  The
degree to which the optical flux affects the observed flux in the
filter will depend on the spectrum of the source.  For a source with a
predominantly optical spectrum, the percentage of light that leaks
into an ultraviolet filter could be significant compared with the
actual ultraviolet flux.  Figure 1 shows the effective area curves of
the <i>Swift</i>/UVOT UVW1 (red) and UVW2 (blue) filters.
</p>
