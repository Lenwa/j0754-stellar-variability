ACTUAL NEW PHOTOMETRY: LEGACY ASAS

The public original ASAS CGI endpoint returned 422 rows in four datasets:
https://www.astrouw.edu.pl/cgi-asas/asas_cgi_get_data?075446+1641.7,asas3
The percent-encoded plus-sign variant returned identical bytes and is a duplicate
snapshot, not another set of observations. Original data: asas_raw.txt.

390 rows have A/B grades and measured MAG_0. They represent 381 distinct HJD/frame
combinations; 12 duplicated HJD/frame groups occur among all raw rows, nine among
the A/B selections. Raw cross-field duplicate entries remain present and marked.
Dataset 2 alone has 338 A/B rows spanning HJD 2452622.71851–2455168.79754,
approximately 2002-12-14–2009-12-03. The other datasets have 3, 1, and 48 A/B rows.
Their measured mean positions lie 1.03–2.23 arcsec from the Gaia coordinate.
No averaging or zero-point adjustment between datasets or surveys was applied.

The provider describes five apertures; MAG_0 is its rule-of-thumb choice at V>=12.
All five magnitudes and frame-dispersion columns are preserved. MER columns are
frame quality statistics, not individual stellar magnitude uncertainties.
29.999 is a below-threshold sentinel without a supplied numeric limiting magnitude;
99.999 means negative aperture flux. Sentinel status is explicit for every aperture.
Grades C/D remain in asas_all_rows.csv but are excluded from asas_mag0_grade_ab.csv.
HJD remains the archive time coordinate; calendar dates are display labels only.

The main field's annual MAG_0 medians include 2004:12.161 (44 points),
2006:12.288 (41), 2007:12.296 (43), 2009:12.175 (31). This descriptive 0.135-mag
shape is visible in asas_legacy_fields.png. It is not yet a comparison-star
controlled astrophysical event. Field-dependent and time-dependent systematics
and blending remain possible. The present retrieval adds genuine coverage to
2002–2009 but does not fill 1989–2002 or 2009–2012.

APPLAUSE: FULL-EPOCH SOURCE AUDIT AND BOUNDED PLATE INVENTORY

A refreshed 15-arcsec radius public cone search, without any epoch or natmag
filter, yielded 41 nearby source rows on 22 plates. Exact Gaia-target matches
provide 26 natural-magnitude rows on 17 physical plates. The unchanged pilot
screen retains nine scan-level rows from six physical plates: 1919-01-05,
1919-03-20, 1952-03-14, and 1971-03-22/26/27. X/Y scans are correlated measurements
of the same plate. The 1952 and 1971 rows were already present in the original
reconnaissance but had not been among the five physical plates examined in pixels;
this audit must not describe them as newly observed archive epochs.

The broadened cone adds one Gaia-matched natural-magnitude row not in the original
smaller cone: plate 93759, 1961-03-17, natmag 13.47268, nominal error 0.21186.
It is beyond the estimated faint calibration limit and at annular bin 9, with
7.28-arcsec Gaia-match distance. It remains rejected, not promoted into photometry.
The 1991-11-12 candidate fails the artifact-score gate (0.723<0.9); the 1954
candidate scores 0.00356. The event plates retain their depth flags. Calibration
outlier status alone is not used as a veto. phot_calib_flags=1 means a valid
calibration star; phot_range_flags=2 means fainter than the estimated plate limit.
No flagged natural magnitude is converted into a validated upper limit.

A direct server-side REGION footprint query returned HTTP 500. The successful
fallback downloaded 6,317 astrometric solutions whose centers lie within 25 deg
of the target, then tested their convex spherical STC polygons offline. This
yielded 331 covering solutions for 295 physical plates. Coverage is geometric,
not a target measurement or a validated non-detection. The search is explicitly
bounded: wide fields with centers farther than 25 deg could remain outside it.

For these 295 plates, 314 exposure records and 1,087 calibration records were
downloaded. All plates have at least one dated exposure; 11 additional exposure
rows have no date. Dated exposure coverage spans 1919-01-05–1991-11-12. Exposure
rows per decade: 1910s 8; 1920s 16; 1930s 151; 1950s 70; 1960s 54; 1970s 3; 1990s 1.
Archive 208 accounts for 241 physical plates. Its calibration rows have median
faint_limit about 12.2 in their natural systems; the target is typically around
13.3 in comparable natural systems. This explains why a large inventory need not
yield useful target photometry, but is not a calibrated local non-detection claim.
All per-plate telescope/emulsion/filter, dates, calibration ranges and target
measurement counts are in applause_covering_plate_metadata.csv. Full metadata
tables and original VOTables are retained. No new plate pixels were measured here.
