        <h4 style="color:red">Notice</h4>
        <ul>
            <li>The charaters comparing in PostgreSQL is case-sensitive, which means class='star' and class='STAR' is not the same. If you want case-insensitive, you could use <span style="font-weight:bold">class <span style="color:red">ilike</span> 'star'</span>.</li>
            <li><a href="http://pgsphere.projects.pgfoundry.org/">pg_Sphere library</a> is available for geometrical search on sphere.</li>
        </ul>
    </div>
    <div style="clear:both; height: 50px;"></div>
        
    <div>
        <h4>Samples</h4>
        <ul>
        <li>Looking for objects that in the circle with center at (331.7 degree, -1.4degress) and radius=0.2 degree.<br>
        <pre>select c.obsid,c.obsdate, c.ra, c.dec, c.z, c.lmjd 
    from catalogue c 
    where spos(c.ra,c.dec) @ scircle '&lt;(331.7d, -1.4d),0.2d&gt;' 
    limit 50
        </pre>
        </li>
        <li>Given certain condition, query the plan list and the information of center stars<br>
        <pre>	
    select obsdate,planid,ra,dec,mjm 
    from plan 
    where obsdate&lt;='2012-12-01' and planid ilike 'GAC%' 
    limit 10
        </pre>
        </li>
        <li>Query the redshift of galaxies during given time<br>
        <pre>select obsdate,planid,spid,fiberid,class,subclass,z 
    from catalogue 
    where obsdate between '20121201' and '20121230' and class ilike 'GALAXY' 
    limit 10
        </pre>
        </li>
        <li>Query stellar atmosphere parameters under certain condition such as date<br>
    <pre>	
    select obsdate,planid,spid,fiberid,rv,logg,feh,teff 
    from stellar 
    where obsdate between '20121201' and '20121230' 
    limit 10
    </pre>	
        </li>
        <li>Query the parameters of F type stars cross-matching between two tables (left connection)<br>
        <pre>select a.obsdate,a.planid,a.spid,a.fiberid,b.rv,logg,feh,teff 
    from catalogue as a 
        left join stellar as b on a.obsid=b.obsid 
    where a.class ilike 'STAR' and a.subclass ilike 'F%' 
    limit 10
        </pre>
        </li>
        <li>Query the parameters of G type stars cross-matching between two tables (fully connected)<br>
        <pre>select a.obsdate,a.planid,a.spid,a.fiberid,b.rv,logg,feh,teff 
    from catalogue as a 
        join stellar as b on a.obsid=b.obsid 
    where a.class ilike 'STAR' and a.subclass ilike 'G%' 
    limit 10
        </pre>
        </li>
        <li>Query the radial velocity and other information of M type stars with H alpha magnetic activity<br>
        <pre>select obsdate,planid,spid,fiberid,rv 
    from mstellar 
    where atcha=1 
    limit 10
        </pre>
        </li>
        </ul>
