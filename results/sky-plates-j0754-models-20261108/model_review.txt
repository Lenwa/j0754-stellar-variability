Material finding: best-fit ranges were narrower than full compatible sets
compare_stellar_models.py originally saved one best tau per partial-covering
nuisance case and one best spot temperature per starting-spot case. summarize()
then retained best fits with max g/W1 residual <=0.02 mag. Those are ranges among
profiled best fits, not the envelope of every sampled physical parameter set
satisfying the same tolerance. The distinction matters for proposed new-color
discrimination. At the nominal atmosphere (5000 K, logg2.5, [M/H]=-0.5), scanning
all dust tau while holding g exactly fixed yields g-Ks changes 0.085-0.148 mag
across the three laws; allowing both g and W1 their +/-0.02 tolerance yields
0.071-0.166 mag. The original primary best-fit summary was only 0.107-0.123 mag.
These are descriptive sampled ranges, not statistical intervals.
Concrete resolution: preserve original best-fit records, additionally enumerate
the declared compatible parameter set in each family, summarize that envelope,
and identify the tolerance and sampling density. Root is implementing this.

  Counts of passing sampled cases are not model probabilities or physical rates.
