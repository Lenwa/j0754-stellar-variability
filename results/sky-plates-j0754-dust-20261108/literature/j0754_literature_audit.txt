Discriminants — our inference, without observing logistics

Near-simultaneous optical and NIR/MIR measurements across bright/faint states test reddening versus temperature/spot changes and thermal dust excess; isolated archival SED points cannot prove variable dust. Multiepoch high-resolution spectra can test 35.92-day RV coherence and line bisectors, refine atmospheric parameters, and measure Li 6708, activity and wind/circumstellar absorption. Polarimetry during fading tests scattering. No warm-IR excess would not exclude cold dust, grey grains or a small covering clump. Photometric coherence at 35.92 d or P/2 would motivate a binary/rotation connection without alone explaining the multiyear behavior.

Only experiment evidence was saved in this literature folder. No maintained source changed. Raw query responses include URLs; the original paper's color, spectral, table and mechanism pages were visually checked.
