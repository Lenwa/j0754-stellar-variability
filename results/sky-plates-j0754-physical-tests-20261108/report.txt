1. H-ALPHA: ACTUAL EQUIVALENT WIDTH AND ERROR PROPAGATION

The primary Gaussian-resolution assumptions are native FWHM7A for300line/mm,
3A for600line/mm, smoothed to10A. The audit's6/2.3A assumptions lie inside
the tested sensitivity grids; they are not known exact per-exposure LSFs.
For linear smoothing/resampling operator A and independent native sigma vector,
C_out=A diag(sigma^2) A^T. Equivalent-width uncertainty includes both the
fitted-continuum gradient and line/continuum cross-covariance. Seeded injections
into actual spectra reproduce the stated conditional noise propagation.

The positive contrast survives all declared LSF, sideband/window, wavelength
and exported-background-rescaling checks. Eighteen continuum/window/degree
variants span.2481–.5197A. Independent LSF variation spans about.448–.514A
in the primary smoothing region. These are conditional sensitivity envelopes,
not confidence limits or exhaustive instrumental-error models.

The audit's mathematical distinction is useful and verified: a broad additive
offset affecting line and continuum gives Wobs=W/(1+alpha), while a continuum-
only multiplicative bias gives deltaW=beta/(1+beta)*(window-W). Using the
actual measurements, a differential continuum-only shift of1.675% could raise
the2009 EW to the2008 value; idealized broad continuum dilution would require
roughly57%. Local emission confined to the line subtracts its integrated
equivalent area. Clean background alone cannot establish stellar activity.
Unknown continuum/LSF/native extraction covariance remain outside formal sigma.
See fast/report.txt, source_sky.png and night_EW.png.

WHAT THIS CHANGES

There is stronger, directly inspected motivation to investigate stellar activity:
a persistent H-alpha product-level difference and a localized flare-like TESS
candidate. Neither establishes that activity drives the slow fading. The present
spectra/colors remain compatible with multiple physical mechanisms. The most
useful missing observation is still state-matched modern spectroscopy plus
simultaneous colors, and independent activity/line-shape-controlled orbital RVs.
