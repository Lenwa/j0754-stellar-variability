The selected-model April2026 longwave shape residual is roughly5.5–6.1% relative to the4.0–4.2um reference, whereas the absolute selected-model longwave excess was~9%. It is not all one gray offset. However all four sampled atmosphere-sensitivity intervals include1, so extra longwave emission is not robustly required by this grid. The ranges are NOT statistical confidence intervals or a completeness proof.

The nuisance grid includes metal−1,−.5,0, logg1–3, temperatures3500–6500K and staticRv3.1 attenuation Av0–1.5. The current grids are coarse plane-parallel LTE models and approximate rectangularpassbands; they cannot validate a detailed low-gravity spherical/nonLTE CO spectrum. No CO emission, CO temperature, dust mass or formal infrared-excess upper limit has been measured. No current result identifies the mechanism of the historical/2018–22 fading.

A future discriminating comparison would use response-convolved spherical giant atmospheres and shared calibration/epoch nuisances, ideally paired with modern spectra and simultaneous optical brightness. The observed shape is worth retaining, not naming as dust or CO prematurely.
